New to USDC on Base? Step by step

  1. Get a wallet app that supports the Base network (for example Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask or Rainbow) and set it up following the app's own instructions. Hustle never needs anything from your wallet except the payment itself.
  2. Get USDC on Base into that wallet. If you hold USDC on an exchange, send it to your wallet address and pick Base as the network. Picking any other network is the most common mistake.
  3. Have a little ETH on Base for the network fee. Most wallets need it to send anything; some cover the fee for you. Your wallet shows the fee before you confirm.
  4. Send the payment. Choose USDC, choose Base, paste the address above, enter at least 9, and compare the first and last six characters of the address before you confirm.
  5. Keep the transaction hash. Your wallet shows it after sending (a long code starting with 0x). You can also find it by searching your own wallet address on basescan.org.
  6. Paid straight from an exchange? Then the sending address is the exchange's, not yours. Open your transaction on basescan.org and use the From address shown there in the order lookup.
  7. Check that it arrived. Every payment to Hustle is public: see the wallet's incoming transfers.
