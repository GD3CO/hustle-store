How buying works

  1. Open a product. Its page shows the table of contents, the price and the payment address.
  2. Send at least that much native USDC on the Base network from a wallet you control. No account or sign-up.
  3. Hustle checks the chain about once an hour and publishes your copy. Find it with the order lookup on the product page.

One payment gets every product priced at or below the amount of that one transfer. Less than a product's price does not get that product.

Every product page has a step-by-step guide for first-time USDC buyers and answers on delivery time, underpayment and refunds (there are none: Hustle cannot send payments).
